FAQ

Questions, answered

IN10C5 is an AI trust layer for digital commerce. Our human-like AI connects brands, businesses and consumers — giving brands real-time insights and faster support, and giving shoppers privacy, verified reviews and a hassle-free post-purchase experience.

Privacy is built in from the start. We follow a zero-party data approach: shoppers own their data, there’s no cross-site tracking, and personal information is never sold to advertising brokers. Our design follows India’s DPDP Act and GDPR principles.

We support common commerce platforms including WooCommerce, Shopify, Magento and BigCommerce, plus a headless API for custom storefronts. If you run something else, contact us and we’ll map the integration.
