Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

We take privacy seriously. This policy explains what personal information we collect on this website, why, and the choices you have.

1.Who we are

This website is operated by IN10C5 India Private Limited, Unit 101, 139 Oxford Towers, HAL Old Airport Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560008, India (“IN10C5”, “we”, “us”). We act as the data fiduciary for the personal information described in this policy.

2.Information we collect

We keep data collection to a minimum. On this website we collect:

  • Information you send us. If you use the “Schedule a consultation” form, we receive your full name, work email, phone number (optional), reason for contact and any message you write.
  • Calculator inputs. The ROI calculator runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter are not sent to us.
  • Technical data from third parties. Our fonts and map are loaded from Google, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers may receive your IP address and browser details when your browser loads their content, under their own privacy policies.

We do not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers on this website.

3.How the contact form works

The form does not store your details on a server. When you press “Send request”, it opens your email app with a pre-filled message addressed to help@in10c5.com. Nothing reaches us until you send that email yourself.

4.How we use your information

We use the information you send us only to:

  • respond to your enquiry and schedule consultations or demos;
  • follow up on the request you made; and
  • meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

5.Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers who help us respond to you (for example, our email provider), and where the law requires it. These providers may only use the information to provide their service to us.

6.How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting relationship, and for as long as the law requires. After that we delete it or anonymise it.

7.Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8.Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other applicable law, you may:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and why;
  • ask us to correct or complete it;
  • ask us to erase it, or withdraw your consent at any time;
  • raise a grievance with us; and
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you are unable to.

To use any of these rights, email help@in10c5.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with the law.

9.Children

This website is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed.

11.Contact us

Questions or complaints about this policy? Write to us at help@in10c5.com or at IN10C5 India Private Limited, Unit 101, 139 Oxford Towers, HAL Old Airport Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560008, India.
